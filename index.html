About Us

TRADING CORPORATION PRIVATE LIMITED is a leading mineral trading company and a pioneer in Odisha, recognized for leveraging state-of-the-art machinery in the trading of minerals and ramming mass. Our advanced processes ensure exceptional quality, consistency, and reliability across every product we deliver.

At IRONFORGE TRADING CORPORATION PRIVATE LIMITED , we are committed to empowering induction furnace users to achieve superior operational outcomes. Our core focus is on maximizing performance, optimizing cost efficiency, and delivering outstanding return on investment (ROI) for our customers.

We understand that manufacturers evaluate refractory solutions based on multiple performance factors. Keeping this in mind, our comprehensive product range is meticulously formulated to perform across diverse operational conditions. From high heat resistance and slag compatibility to extended lining life, our products are engineered to deliver dependable results in demanding production environments.

Quality, innovation, and customer-centricity form the foundation of our operations. By combining technical expertise with modern infrastructure, we ensure that our solutions meet the evolving needs of the mining and manufacturing industries.

Our leadership plays a vital role in guiding the company’s growth and vision. Under the direction of our experienced leadership team, IRONFORGE continues to strengthen its position as a trusted partner in the mineral and refractory sector.
